Eligibility Criteria

  • ►Child must meet the minimum age requirement for the stage applied for as of June 1st of the entry year.
  • ►Students transferring from another school must provide the last two years of academic records and a school leaving certificate.
  • ►English proficiency is expected for Stages 3 and above. Admissions staff will assess language readiness during the campus visit.
  • ►Students with special learning needs are welcome. Please disclose relevant information during the inquiry stage so we can assess support arrangements.
  • ►Admission to Upper Secondary (IGCSE) requires satisfactory performance in Lower Secondary. Internal students are given priority placement.
  • ►All admissions are subject to seat availability at the time of application.
