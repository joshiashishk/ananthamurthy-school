Our Programmes

Where Cambridge rigour meets Kerala's living traditions.

Ages 3–6

Kindergarten

Our early years programme follows the Cambridge International Early Years framework, nurturing children's natural curiosity through experiential discovery, play, and cooperative learning.

  • ► LKG 1 (Ages 3–4), LKG 2 (Ages 4–5), UKG (Ages 5–6)
  • ► Letters and Sounds phonics programme (6 phases)
  • ► Social-emotional learning and motor skill development
  • ► Kalarippayattu and Carnatic music from UKG

Traditional Martial Art

Kalarippayattu

One of the world's oldest martial arts, Kalarippayattu is not an optional extra at Ananthamurthy — it is part of the core curriculum for every student from UKG onwards. It builds discipline, physical confidence, and deep pride in Kerala's warrior heritage.

  • ► Mandatory for all students from UKG / Stage 1
  • ► In partnership with Madhava Matam CVN Kalari Ayurvedic Centre
  • ► Builds physical fitness, mental focus, and body awareness
  • ► Rooted in the ancient traditions of Kerala

Carnatic & Western

Music

Music at Ananthamurthy bridges two worlds — the classical Carnatic tradition of South India and Western music traditions. Students develop musical literacy, ear training, and performance skills.

  • ► Carnatic classical vocal — led by Kavalam Sajeev
  • ► Western music — led by Jef Lean
  • ► Tie-up with Kavalam School of Music
  • ► All students from UKG participate

Cultural Immersion

Keraleeyam

Keraleeyam is the school's cultural immersion programme — a deep, experiential exploration of Kerala's heritage, language, performing arts, and natural environment. It is what makes Ananthamurthy uniquely rooted.

  • ► Malayalam language and literature
  • ► Field visits to cultural sites across Kerala
  • ► Visits to institutions like Kerala Kalamandalam
  • ► Performing arts, crafts, and seasonal celebrations

Special Initiative

CLAP — Creative Language Acquisition Programme

CLAP is a 10-day intensive programme for students in Grades 3–6, designed to build language confidence through drama, storytelling, debate, and creative writing. Students emerge with sharper communication skills and greater confidence in English expression.
