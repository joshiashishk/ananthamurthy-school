Our Story

A Different Kind of School

Ananthamurthy International School was founded with a clear conviction: that the best education gives children both the global competencies to succeed in the world and the cultural identity to know who they are.

Located in Vattiyoorkavu, Thiruvananthapuram, the school serves students from age 3 to 19 through a Cambridge International pathway, enriched by Kerala's living traditions — Kalarippayattu, Carnatic music, Malayalam literature, and the spirit of Keraleeyam.

In March 2021, the school was formally approved as a Cambridge International Affiliated School — a recognition of its rigorous academic standards and commitment to holistic child development.
